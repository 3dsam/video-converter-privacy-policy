Privacy Policy for Video Converter

Last Updated: October 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Video Converter ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our Android mobile application ("Application").

1. Local Processing & Media Privacy

  • Local Conversion: All video and audio conversion processes occur entirely on your local device.
  • No Media Uploads: Your media files, video contents, audio streams, and output files are never uploaded, transmitted, or stored on external servers or cloud services.

2. Information We Collect Automatically

A. Crash & Diagnostic Data (Firebase Crashlytics)

To ensure app stability and fix crashes, we use Firebase Crashlytics (provided by Google LLC). When a crash occurs, the app may collect anonymous diagnostic data, including:

  • Device state information (operating system version, device orientation, available memory)
  • Stack traces and crash details
  • Pseudonymous installation identifiers

This data is used solely to identify and fix bugs and cannot be used to personally identify you.

B. Advertising Data (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to serve advertisements within the Application. AdMob may collect and process information such as:

  • Pseudonymous advertising identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location data
  • Device hardware specifications and performance metrics
  • User interaction data with displayed advertisements

This information is processed according to Google's Privacy & Terms.

3. App Permissions

The Application requests the following permissions for core functionality:

  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PROCESSING / DATA_SYNC: Required to ensure video processing finishes smoothly in the background without being killed by the OS.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used to notify you when a video conversion task completes or encounters an error.
  • WAKE_LOCK: Used during active video rendering to prevent the CPU from sleeping mid-conversion.

4. Third-Party Services Privacy Policies

Our application integrates third-party services that have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Firebase Crashlytics

5. Children's Privacy

Our Application is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Your Rights & Choices

  • Ad Personalization: You can reset or opt-out of personalized advertising at any time through your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).
  • Permissions Control: You can revoke app permissions (such as Notifications) at any time through your Android system settings.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
